Band 01 — Industry-Relevant ICT

The Carrier Wave

ICT Track Sessions

Every transmission needs infrastructure before content. Setting up your environment is not overhead — it is the medium through which all your work will travel. The tools are not the work; they are what make the work possible.

Using Git & GitHub

Version control is not just a safety net — it is a record of your reasoning. Building OmniSense taught me this firsthand: every commit message is a small transmission to your future self and your collaborators. Write it clearly, because someone will read it.

Band 02 — Community Engagement & Scholarship

The Broadcast Tower

Mozilla Global Assemblies ★

This was the session that changed my frequency entirely. Technology exists inside a civic ecosystem. The question shifted from "what can I build?" to "who is listening, and what do they need to hear?"

This reframe brought our team's idea to life: a WhatsApp chatbot grounded in Kenya's Employment Act 2007 — built for young workers who do not know their rights exist. A human need first. Technology second.
Contributing to Open Source

A transmission is only valuable if others can receive it. Open source is collective signal infrastructure — documentation fixes, code cleanups, and issue reports are the repeaters that keep the whole network strong. Contribution is not always authorship.

Band 03 — Professional Development

Signal Modulation

Storytelling & Writing

A story without a hook is a signal without a carrier — it dissipates before it reaches anyone. The opening line determines whether your message is received or simply scrolled past.

Storytelling in Speech

Structure is your waveform. Emotion is your frequency. The arc from problem to inspiring solution is what gives a speech its shape and reach.

Public Speaking in Practice

Confidence is not volume — it is clarity. Breath control, grounded posture, and intentional eye contact are transmission parameters, not performance tricks.

Band 04 — Personal Development & Innovation

Noise Cancellation

Work & Time Management

Time management is not about fitting more in — it is about protecting what matters most. Pomodoro is duty cycling: work in bursts, recover between them. Saying "no" is a high-pass filter that removes low-value signals from your bandwidth before they consume it.

Principles of Innovation

Real innovation begins with a diagnosis, not a solution. The pain point comes first. Our Mozilla chatbot did not start from "let's use RAG" — it started from a truth: young Kenyan workers do not know their rights, and the law is locked inside a document they will never read.
